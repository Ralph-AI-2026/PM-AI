# Riley Archery Range - City of Farmington Hills, MI

This uniquely designed open air (indoor/outdoor) community archery range offers eight shooting lanes with shooting distances of 10 - 30 yards, providing programs and shooting opportunities for people of all ages and abilities, allowing both bows and crossbows. Riley Archery Range was made possible with support from the Riley Foundation, Panasonic, Safari Club International Novi-Chapter, ROWE Professional Services, and Michigan DNR. Several organizations and businesses have partnered with Farmington Hills Archery, including Field & Stream, Camp Liberty, and others, providing guests a unique experience at a community archery range.

Riley Archery Range offers Open Shooting Hours (days/times vary by season); Beginner and Intermediate Classes; Leagues; Group Lessons for scouts, corporate groups and others; Private Lessons; Private Range Rental; Camps; Birthday Parties; Special Events, Competitions and more.

### Things to know:

  * Your one-hour time slot includes set up and clean up time. Please select the time and date that you would like to reserve ONLINE.
  * Remember to add equipment rental to your reservation. A $5 fee will be charged per person for equipment or guests are welcome to bring your own.
  * Each archer will need to reserve their own lane. Lane or equipment sharing is not permitted.
  * To reserve a time slot for multiple people, please add each individual as a member of your household account (remember to add date of birth for each person).
  * The shooting area is covered, but the range is OUTDOORS. Dress for the weather



### Fees

  * Youth (17 & under) / Seniors 55+ - $7/hour
  * Adults - $8/hour
  * Equipment Rental (bow/3 arrows) - $5/hour (or bring your own equipment)



Not sure how to shoot? Range Masters can assist you with our 1st Shot Demo Program - 5 minute teaching time/demonstration during any Open Shooting time, or sign up for an Introduction to Archery Class or Beginner Class.
