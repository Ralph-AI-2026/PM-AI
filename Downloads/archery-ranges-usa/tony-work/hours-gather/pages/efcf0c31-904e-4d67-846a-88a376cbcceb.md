# Home | Lamprey River Bowhunters, New Hampshire archery, Brentwood, NH

LRB is a private archery club located at the Brentwood, NH Recreation Center.

​

[Our facilities ](https://www.lampreyriverbowhunters.com/_files/ugd/bd0139_7cff333f8fe84d2d9d976439c90ef888.pdf)include a practice range with targets from 20 to 50 yards and a 15-target 3D range along a one mile woods trail.

We are a family friendly club. [Membership](https://www.lampreyriverbowhunters.com/_files/ugd/bd0139_62312a77189c444f98123f3aca3d9d93.pdf) is open to anyone interested in archery.

While many members are bowhunters, many are not and simply enjoy the sport of archery.

The club is open to members from sunrise to sunset, year-round.

We have both traditional and compound bow archers.

We host two [Granite State Bowhunter (GSB)](http://www.granitestatebowhunters.com/) 30 target 3D shoots per year.
