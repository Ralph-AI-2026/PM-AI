# Long Beach State Archery Club - Home

## Welcome to the Long Beach State Archery Club

The Long Beach State Archery Club (LBSAC) is one of the longest running collegiate archery programs in the country. LBSAC encourages the growth of the sport of archery by promoting archery skills, sportsmanship, and comradery. Whether your coming to shoot for competition or for fun, everyone is welcome to come join us and enjoy the world of archery.
