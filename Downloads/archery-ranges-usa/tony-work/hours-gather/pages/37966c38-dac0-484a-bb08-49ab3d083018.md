# OCSC Archery - Home

## About Our Archery Club 

Welcome to the Archery Club Website at Oakland County Sportsmen’s Club.

We are proud to be a family oriented club that truly has something for everyone of all ages and abilities.

We have a beautiful clubhouse with a large indoor range as well as acres of outdoor ranges located on beautiful rolling, wooded, and open field terrain.

We have a great variety of leagues ongoing year round. We also host a variety of events, 3D shoot both indoors and outdoors and tournaments. Remember you do not need to be a member to shoot in any of the leagues or other events. You are always welcome to shoot as a guest.

As a member you have access to the indoor range 24 / 7 and the outdoor ranges during daylight hours. Just remember to check first for any range closings and to sign in to use the ranges.

If you have a special event you would like to schedule, such as corporate team building day, scout instructional or fun shoot, see one of the archery club representatives to arrange. We can even provide equipment and instruction. 

Archery Club meetings are held the third Wednesday of the month. All club members are welcome to attend and get involved. 

Sign up for e-mail notices (on the Homepage) to get the latest information on upcoming leagues and special events.

Come out and get involved in this great sport of Archery!
