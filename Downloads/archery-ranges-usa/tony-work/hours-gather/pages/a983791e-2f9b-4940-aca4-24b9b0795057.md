# Golden Gate JOAD Archery Club - Bringing Bay Area adults and kids into the exciting sport of archery

We are an all-volunteer club that teaches recreational archery to kids and adults in San Francisco. We are based at the [Golden Gate Park Archery Range](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kpeb9CSsqhk244Td7). We support our archers by offering coaching, club archery equipment on loan, regular scored shoots where archers can earn achievement pins, advanced instruction in recurve archery form using the National Training System, league competitions, and special events like our annual Halloween shoot.

### **How do I join?**

All prospective members must take a new archer lesson with us. These can fill up quickly and there is often a waitlist. Please note that the minimum age for our beginner lessons is 8 years old. For the next lesson date, please see the calendar.  


If you cannot wait for the next available new archer lesson, we recommend the SF Rec and Park archery program or the San Francisco Archery Shop on the corner of 39th Ave. & Balboa St. in the Richmond District.

### **Do you offer private lessons?**

We do not offer private lessons at this time. We are an all-volunteer club and as such our teaching capacity is limited to regularly scheduled beginner lessons.

### **What do you with your lesson fees?**

We replace our equipment as it gets old, buy uniforms (and the occasional pizza) for our coaches, and purchase materials to replace the Golden Gate Park Archery field bales. In recent years we've been grateful to see the city put time and labor into archery field maintenance, but we still help with volunteers and materials.
