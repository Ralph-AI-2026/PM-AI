# Eros Archery - Waterbury, CT - Home

Eros Archery is an archery range and retail space located in Thomaston, CT. We offer youth lessons, homeschool lessons, and private lessons given by coaches who are certified by USA Archery and Safe Sport.

  


In our retail space, you will find Sebastian Flute and Fivics Olympic recurves and accessories, Elite bows, CBE accessories, Altra arrows, Easton arrows and products, Gas bowstrings, and 8B+ chalk bags and release pounces. We also stock all shooting accessories, including armguards, quivers, stabilizers, and more. Finally, we carry pocket knives from Civivi, Kershaw, CRKT, and more, as well as plenty of novelty archery items.

  


Whether you’re just developing an interest in archery or are a seasoned marksman, we think you’ll love spending time at Eros Archery.
