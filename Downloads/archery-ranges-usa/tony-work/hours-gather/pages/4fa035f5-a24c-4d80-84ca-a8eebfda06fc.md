> _**3-D Shoot's**_
> 
> **They will be held on the**
> 
> **4th Sunday****of each Month.**
> 
> **_It’s been brought to the boards attention on more than one occasion that there were some safety concerns with our monthly 3D shoots. Due to these concerns the board has made the following changes to our monthly shoot program._**
> 
>   
>  **_*Sign ups for each shoot will be between 8am-8:50a.  
>    
>  *All participants must attend the 9am shooter safety meeting before the shoot begins. This is where we explain the course and any safety info to be shared for the round.  
>    
>  *Following the safety meeting, shooters will be put in groups of 3-5 and assigned a target for a shotgun start. The course must be shot in numeric sequence from your assigned target. (No cutting the course or skipping targets) Therefore no shooters will be allowed on the course by themselves or after the shoot has begun so we can control the pace and safety of the active shooting range. _**  
> 
> 
> _**Price for participatimg Is $15, for_** _members or nonmembers_**** _This also includes lunch.**_**
