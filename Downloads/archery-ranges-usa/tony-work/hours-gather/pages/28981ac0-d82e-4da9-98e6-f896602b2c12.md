# Manistique Rifle & Pistol Club | Trap & Skeet

Come and join us at the Manistique Rifle and Pistol Club!  
We are located 8 miles north of Manistique on Highway M-94.  
We are open to all shooting sports enthusiasts of all ages!

Hours: Every Wednesday, from 2:00 - closing.
