#### Welcome to Salina Bowmen, Inc.

We offer monthly 3D shoots (April - October) on a beautiful 30 acre wooded site. Shooting range offers target bag range from 10 -50 yards. Burlap targets with various wildlife targets are placed over the 30 acres. Members can enjoy shooting 365 days per year. Please contact us with your questions.

**Mission Statement:**

To promote the sport of archery, with continuing education and practicing of traditions being the foundation that will establish the qualities all archers and hunters should incorporate. To insure that future generations enjoy the privilege of shooting and hunting   
with a bow and arrow.

SALINA BOWMEN HISTORICAL NOTES 

Here is a short summary of the Salina Bowmen’s past:

The Salina Bowmen Archery Club was first organized forty three years ago in February 1961. The Club’s first bow range was on land leased from the George Will farm on Mulberry Creek, about five miles west of Salina. This range was quickly damaged by flood.

In June 1961, the range was moved to a 28 acre lease at Camp Webster, just north of Salina on the Saline River.

The range was moved again in March 1963 to a leased wooded area on the north side of the Smoky Hill River, about three miles east of Salina.

In March 1971, the Salina Bowmen purchased the land of the present bow range, a 30 acre wooded site about a mile southeast of Niles, in Ottawa County. This land parcel is described as: N 30 Acres SE1/4 SW1/4 Sec 32 T12S R1W, Ottawa County, KS.
