## Welcome to Square1 Outdoors, Inc.

### We are a 501(c)(3) non-profit ALL VOLUNTEER corporation dedicated to the promoting outdoor activities for youth and families in Northeast Mississippi.

Our organization was incorporated in **2012** by Kevin Owens for the purpose of promoting outdoor activities for youth and families and for supporting existing programs with related purposes, such as Mississippi Hunter & Boater Education Programs, scouting (at the unit level), 4H shooting sports, and to facilitate non-traditional outdoor sporting activities of interest to youth and families.

We are an all-volunteer organization. **No one receives a salary.** We do not pay professional fundraisers to raise money for us. Our dedicated volunteers include men and women from all walks of life, including parents, veterans, emergency responders, and others who share their passion for outdoor pursuits with adults and youth who participate in our shooting club and other activities.

Square1 Outdoors, Inc. has two properties. **Sullivan Farms Recreational Shooting Area** is located near Tupelo, Mississippi and it is home to our outdoor shooting ranges. **The Prentiss County****Sportsman’s Club** is located near Booneville, Mississippi and it is home to our indoor air gun and archery ranges, as well as outdoor archery and shotgun shooting. It also serves as a our base camp for water safety and small craft paddling. Outdoor ranges are in the works for the Sportsman’s Club.
