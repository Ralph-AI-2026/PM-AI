# MinaraABQ.org - Minara Muslim Community Center

[![](https://minaraabq.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/donate02.png)](https://minaraabq.org/donate/)[Donate to Minara](https://minaraabq.org/donate/)

Welcome to the MINARA Muslim Community Center, a dedicated Islamic center located in the heart of Albuquerque, New Mexico. Our center serves as a welcoming space for both local and visiting Muslims. It aims to foster strong community ties, provide religious guidance, and support individuals in their spiritual journey. MINARA is a 501(c)3 nonprofit organization dedicated to serving the Albuquerque community in the areas of Homeless Feeding, Islamic Da’wah, Health & Wellness, Language Arts & Calligraphy, Martial Arts (Judo) and Archery Classes. Stay up to date on [MINARA Facebook](http://facebook.com/MinaraABQ)!
