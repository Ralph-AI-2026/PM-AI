At Lifetime Archery, we use the NTS (National Training System) method, the same method used by the medal-winning archers from the USA Olympic Archery Team. Whether you are shooting archery just for fun or are the type of archer who likes competing in tournaments, the NTS method provides a clear and proven method to improve shot consistency, increase endurance and get the most enjoyment out of the sport... important results for any archer! 

Coach John trains and re-certifies in the NTS method at the US Olympic Training Center and has earned the Level 4 NTS certification since 2008\. Level 4 NTS Coaches can only be certified by the Olympic Head Coach, Kisik Lee.
