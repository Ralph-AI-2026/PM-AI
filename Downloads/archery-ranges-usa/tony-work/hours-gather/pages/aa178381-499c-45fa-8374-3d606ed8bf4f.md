# Archery Club | Walker River Bowmen | Lyon County, NV

With 22 lanes extending up to 30 yards and an additional lane reaching 35 yards, members have ample opportunity to practice and hone their skills. The range is accessible 24/7 to members who wish to train at their convenience.

  
From January to April, the range hosts a variety of different groups: Thursday nights are dedicated to the Adult Indoor League, providing a competitive yet social atmosphere for adult archers. Friday evenings focus on youth archery, fostering a supportive environment for younger participants to learn and improve.  
  


For the rest of the week, the range is set up for Indoor 3D, offering a dynamic and challenging experience to prepare for outdoor 3D tournaments and hunting scenarios.
