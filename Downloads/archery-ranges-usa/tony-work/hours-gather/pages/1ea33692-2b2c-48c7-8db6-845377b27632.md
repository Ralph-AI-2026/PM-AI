Level 2 Instructor Certification

The USA Archery Level 2 Instructor Certification Course provides graduates with the necessary tools to serve as a lead instructor for on-going archery programs such as local club and school programs, adult archery programs, and Junior Olympic Archery Development (JOAD). It also provides graduates with an introduction to the National Training System (NTS) Steps of Shooting for both recurve and compound bows, equipment and accessory setup, athlete development, and archery events.

Custom Programs

Our custom programs offer a fun and interactive team-building activity, perfect for your corporate team, family gathering or school group. Maine School of Archery will introduce your group members of all ages and abilities to recurve bare-bow archery. Archers will learn proper form and shoot arrows together from the shooting line. Our light-weight training bows are ideal for both the young and young at heart members in your group. Let us craft a unique archery experience that aligns with your group’s size and objectives.

Level 3 Coach Certification

The USA Archery Level 3 Coach Certification Course provides candidate with the tools to move beyond group instruction and focus on individual athlete development. Students will build a deeper understanding of evidence-based coaching methods, the National Training System for both recurve and compound bows, movement analysis, feedback methods, mental process, training theory, athlete development, and coaching philosophy. This certification is a strong next step for instructors working with club archers, JOAD programs, competitive archers, or developing athletes who need more individualized coaching and structured training. Graduates will be better prepared to design effective practice sessions, analyze shooting form, and support long-term athlete progress.

Level 1 Instructor Certification

The USA Archery Level 1 Instructor Certification Course provides graduates with the necessary tools to maintain a high level of safety both on and off the archery range for camps and beginner archery programs. The Level 1 instructor will learn about range safety and setup, the steps of shooting a bow, equipment and repair, programs and lesson plans, and will be well prepared to teach beginner archery programs to people of all ages and abilities.
