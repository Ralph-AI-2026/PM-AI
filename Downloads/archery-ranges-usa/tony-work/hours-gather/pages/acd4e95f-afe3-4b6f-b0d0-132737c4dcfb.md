# SiWen Archery homepage | Si Wen Learning Center

**Location:** 8855 Woodbury Rd, Laingsburg, MI 48848.

**Time:** 1:30 p.m. – 3:00 p.m

**Start Date:** March 15, 2026

**Format:** Ongoing weekly class

**Class Information:**

This is a small group form clinic session (maximum 6 archers per session). Each 1.5-hour session ensures that every archer receives approximately 15 minutes of one-on-one coaching.

Bring your questions — we will discuss them together and work toward solutions. The coach will also provide individual training guidance and assessment.

**Class Includes:**

1\. Group warm-up

2\. Question sharing and discussion

3\. Structured group training (if there are common form-related topics)

4\. Individual one-on-one coaching

5\. Session summary and key takeaways

The session will be canceled if enrollment is fewer than three archers.

**Fee:**

Single session: $30

You may also use your punch card if you have previously purchased a class plan.

Registration is required at least 48 hours before the class.
