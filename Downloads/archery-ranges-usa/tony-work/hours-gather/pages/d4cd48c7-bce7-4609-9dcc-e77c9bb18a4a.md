# Our Instructors - Folksy Farmstead

_L4 USA Archery NTS Coach, L2 Instructor Trainer, Certified 4-H Coach, Competitive Barebow & Compound Archer_

In addition to being a competitive barebow and compound archer, Emmie coaches all bow types and age divisions, bringing a strong knowledge of form development, equipment adjustment, and tournament prep to the archers she serves. She loves helping new archers develop a passion for the sport, and established archers reach new levels of competition. As an archer, her most recent achievements include earning the titles of Collegiate Indoor National Champion, Highest Scoring Female Barebow at The Vegas Shoot, and Barebow Champion at the US Qualifier Salt Lake Summitt. Emmie is currently on scholarship at William Carey University and serves as an assistant coach.
