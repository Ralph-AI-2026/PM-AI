# The Pootatuck Archers

# An Ancient Sport for Modern Times

Archery is an ancient skill that has grown from its survival and military roots into an enjoyable, modern sport. Located in beautiful Redding, Connecticut, our range offers a variety of targets for many different styles. Whether you shoot paper targets, field archery, 3-D targets, or hunt, the Pootatuck Archers is the club for you.  
  
The Pootatuck Archers are currently accepting new members. If you are interested in joining, please check out our Membership page for more information.
