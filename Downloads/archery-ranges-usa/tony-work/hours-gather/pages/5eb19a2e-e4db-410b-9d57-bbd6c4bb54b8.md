# Sheridan County Sportsmen's Association | Shooting Range Archery & Gun Club

SCSA encourages organized rifle, pistol, shotgun, black powder, and archery shooting among the members of our community, with a view toward a better knowledge of safe gun handling and proper care of firearms as well as improved marksmanship. SCSA promotes the characteristics of honesty, good fellowship, self-discipline, team play, and self-reliance as the essentials of good sportsmanship.
