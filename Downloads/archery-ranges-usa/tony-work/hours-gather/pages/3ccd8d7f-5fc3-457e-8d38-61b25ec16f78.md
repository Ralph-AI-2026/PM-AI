# Welcome to Lincoln Bowmen Archery Club

Lincoln Bowmen Archery Club is a non-profit organization founded in 1946 and dedicated to the advancement of the sport of archery. We strive to nurture existing archers, cultivate the new archer and advance the sport through camaraderie and sportsmanship.

Lincoln Bowmen offers an inviting clubhouse on 40 acres of land, which includes two wooded 3D courses, a broadhead range, as well as indoor and outdoor target ranges. We have programs for the entire family, such as an instructional [Archery Classes (YAP)](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/yap), [High School Archery](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/lbhsa) and various [Leagues](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/archeryleagues), including target, traditional and 3D leagues.

The club hosts various festive [3D shoots](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/shoots) as well as [competitive shoots](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/shoots) sanctioned at the state and national level by [organizations](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/organizations) such as the [IAA](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/iaa), [MAA](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/maa), [MET](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/met), [NFAA](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/nfaa) and [USAA](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/naa). In addition to shoots the club hosts annual family events that include our famous [Pig Roast Dinner](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/pigroast) and [Breakfast with Santa](https://www.lincolnbowmen.org/santa).
