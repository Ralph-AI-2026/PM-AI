# Kishwaukee Archers Inc.

Kishwaukee Archers is a family friendly, non-profit archery sports club located in Sycamore Illinois. 

Amenities include: 

  * A heated indoor range allows for all season shooting in comfort at distances up to 30 yards with 18 lanes.

  * An outdoor practice range features targets from 10 to 100 yards, an elevated tower stand and lights. 

  * A full field course winds through the woods on our 20+ acre property.

  * A 3D course and hunter practice range are also available.

  * The rustic clubhouse adjoins the indoor range and has a full kitchen, a lounge area with a cozy fireplace, and an outdoor picnic area.

  * No hunting at our club.




The club is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year for our members to enjoy.  
  
The club hosts many events throughout the year, including national and state sanctioned tournaments, target and field leagues, kids’ leagues, youth programs, archery training, and both indoor and outdoor 3D shoots.   
  
**Kishwaukee Archers club maintains affiliation with: USA Archery  
National Field Archery Association (NFAA)  
Archery Shooters Association (ASA)  
Midwest Archery Championship (MAC)  
Illinois Target Archery Association (ITAA)  
Field Archery of Illinois (FAI)**
