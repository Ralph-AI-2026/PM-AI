# KBH Archers - Home

Discover the premier archery experience on the Kitsap Peninsula at KBH Archers — an inclusive club open to all skill levels and ages. Spanning 34 acres, our facility offers unparalleled opportunities for archery enthusiasts. 

## World-Class Facilities

Two outdoor ranges for just $10/person during daylight hours, a 28-target walk-through range, flat range with targets up to 100 yards, and a dedicated broadhead area. Members enjoy exclusive access to our 5,000 sq. ft. clubhouse, 18-lane indoor range, and personal gate code. 

## Vibrant Events & Community

Exciting 3D shoots, WSAA and NFAA tournaments, multicolor and blueface leagues, field rounds, and educational events. Beginners and seasoned archers alike thrive in our welcoming, vibrant community. 

## Camping & Tournaments

On-site dry camping for $10/night (free for tournament participants — first-come, first-served). Our expansive grounds make KBH the ultimate destination for multi-day archery adventures. 

## Relax in Nature

Sometimes the greatest reward is simply soaking in the peaceful beauty of our 34-acre property surrounded by tranquil woods.
