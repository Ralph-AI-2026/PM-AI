# Land-O-Lakes Bowmen - Home

## Fenton Michigan's Best Kept Secret!

Land-O-Lakes Bowmen is a family and friendship oriented archery club that was founded in 1954. The grounds consist of 44 mostly wooded acres with undulating terrain and 4 nature surrounded walking trail shooting courses which feature both 3D and broadhead targets. Our ranges and courses feature mostly all Rinehart targets and are designed to replicate real hunting situations. Shooters will be challenged with a variety of target conditions including uphill shots and elevated platforms.  
  
We are located off US-23 just south of the city of Fenton in the great state of Michigan.   
​We also offer 65 primitive campground sites open to active members year around.
