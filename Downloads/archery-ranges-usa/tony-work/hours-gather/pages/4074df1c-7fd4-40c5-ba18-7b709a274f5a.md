# Panther Creek Bowhunters Club

Monthly events are **OPEN TO THE PUBLIC** and family friendly.

Our 30 Target 3D course runs along a one mile trail through beautiful forested bottomlands near the Panther and Sugar Creek junction.

Pegs are set for each of the following classes.

Cub  
Youth  
Traditional  
Women  
Bowhunter - No rangefinders or movable sights  
Known Distance - Rangefinders and Movable sights allowed  
Open/Pro - Unknown Distance - No Range Finders

The 18 target Tac course is broken into two sections. This course will test your skills and equipment with challenging long range shots up to 80+ yards. Rangefinders and movable sights are recommended.  
TAC $5

All participants are welcome to shoot the practice range with targets at 5 yard increments from 10-80y.

Don't miss out on our novelty shoots to further test your skills and compete for prizes.

**Registration from 8am-1pm**

**Adults $12 - Youth $8 - Family $30**

**TAC $5**
