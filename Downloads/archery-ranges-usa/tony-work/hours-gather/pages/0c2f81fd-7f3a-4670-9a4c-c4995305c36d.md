# Flint Bowmen | Archery Range Michigan | Flint Bowmen Inc, Belsay Road, Grand Blanc, MI, USA

Flint Bowmen has been passionately promoting the sport of archery since 1937, that's 87 years of archery history. Whether you're a novice or a pro, Flint Bowmen has something to offer everyone. We have 28 acres to utilize for setting fun and challenging 3D courses, We have a 14 target field and hunter course, an outdoor practice range with bales in 5 yard increments from 5 - 65 yards. The indoor facility is 30 yards in length and has 14 lanes. This member - run club is for archers run by archers. Flint Bowmen hosts many events during the year from leagues, to smackdowns, to indoor spot tournaments, to 3D tournaments indoors and out. There are indoor leagues in the winter and fall, and 3D leagues in spring and summer. Whether you're a lifelong archer or just discovering your love for the sport, you'll have a home at Flint Bowmen. So, bring out the family and enjoy learning this challenging and fun sport of archery together.
