# Guan Ho Ha Fish and Game Club - Home

# Welcome to the Guan Ho Ha Fish and Game Club, Inc.

_**Our Mission**_

The prime objective shall be the conservation of game, game fish, and waterfowl and related habitat, by having respect for Federal and State game laws; to conform to the attitude of true sportsmanship; to offer hunter training instruction in the safe use of archery and firearms to citizens; and to support the preservation for the right of all law-abiding citizens to purchase, possess, and use firearms for legitimate purposes as guaranteed by the Second Amendment of the Constitution of the United States of America.
