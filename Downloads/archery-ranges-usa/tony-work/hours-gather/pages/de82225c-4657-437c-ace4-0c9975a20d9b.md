# HEART OF MARYLAND BOWHUNTERS

During the spring and summer months the club sponsors monthly 30-target 3D shoots normally on the first Sunday of the month but occasionally on a later Sunday to avoid holiday weekends. In 2025, we will have a couple of Saturday afternoon shoots and a couple of Saturday evening spotlight shoots. All of these shoots are open to the public.

All members get their Sunday shoots for free, non-members pay $15. 

Most of our targets are Rinehart targets and all are in excellent condition. Our 30-target 3D course, which is separate from the three members-only practice ranges, is a 3/4 mile long loop with varying terrain and many uphill and downhill shots as well as shots from four elevated platforms. 

See the calendar for all specific shoot dates.
