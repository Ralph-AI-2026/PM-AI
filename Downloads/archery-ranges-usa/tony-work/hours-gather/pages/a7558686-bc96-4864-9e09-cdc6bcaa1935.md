# Archery | Chia Archery | Castro Valley

About CHIA ARCHERY  
  
CHIA ARCHERY, LLC is a high-performance archery club and a Consulting Service business. 

CHIA ARCHERY offers JOAD archery training for U10, U13, U15, U18, U21, and Young Adults. You can join our club as a competitive archer or as a recreational archer. Everyone receives the same training. We encourage and support recreational archers to compete at local and charity competitions once they gain confidence in their archery skills. 

We cohabitate with Ohlone Archery, 2301 Verna Court, San Leandro, CA 94577 for indoor training, and we practice outdoors at select archery ranges. Our club supports Junior Olympic Archery Development (JOAD) archers aged 5 to young adults, coaches competitive adult archers, and offers USA Archery Practical certification for Level 1, 2, and 3-NTS.

​

Joining an archery club is important to practice archery in a safe and fun environment and to be surrounded by archers with equal or greater experience than you. We are an active and enthusiastic archery club with events and activities taking place year-round. We strive to maintain the energy and excitement while we practice. 

We train and travel together to State and National ranking tournaments. Our club has successfully coached archers to achieve the Minimum Qualification Score (MQS) for USA Archery's Regional Elite and National Elite programs and to be accepted into the USA Archery team for U18, U21, and as an Olympic Resident Athlete. 

CHIA ARCHERY is your stepping stone to achieving your Olympic dream.

Our coaching method adheres to the National Training System (NTS). The advantage of the NTS includes its biomechanical efficiency, which results in less fatigue and injury. The archer benefits from having a shooting process that can be consistently applied under tournament pressure. We believe technique translates to score.

​

Our CHIA ARCHERY family is a strong social group of parents and archers; both parents and archers are evaluated for membership. Together, we cry and celebrate our archer's successes. Many club parents have become certified as USA Archery Level 2-NTS, Level 3-NTS, and Level 4-NTS coaches, serving as a coaching multiplier to enhance our archery programs. We invite and welcome you to join our archery family. ​
