Oakridge has been a proud member of the community since 1990, dedicated to inspiring a lasting interest in archery and bow hunting. As a non-profit organization, we strive to make the sport accessible and enjoyable for everyone—from beginners to seasoned archers—through education, community engagement, and hands-on experience. Our welcoming environment fosters skill development, sportsmanship, and a deep respect for nature, while helping members form meaningful connections with others who share their passion.

Every other Sunday, we offer 3D target shoots that provide a fun and challenging experience for archers of all skill levels. In addition, we host both summer and winter archery leagues to keep our members active and engaged year-round. Starting in 2025, we’re proud to begin offering USA Archery JOAD (Junior Olympic Archery Development) programs for youth, as well as USA Archery programs for adults—further expanding our commitment to high-quality instruction and competitive development.
