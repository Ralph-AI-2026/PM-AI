### Taekwondo

Our Taekwondo program teaches children and teens the benefits of the Kukkiwon Taekwondo style, giving them a focus on life. We emphasize Respect, Humility, Self Control, Perseverance, and Honesty. Our classes are for children aged 6 and above.

## Li’l Abner Foundation is a not-for-profit 501C organization, that offers the Citizens of Miami Dade County , Florida a FREE Tutoring, dance, Taekwondo & Archery programs for children from 1st through 12th grade.

Our Goal is Improve the lives of all residents in the communities where we operate.

We believe and support that education and sports are very important for the development of the children of our community, because this is not the generation of the future but the generation of the present, that can change the future.

## Our Programs

If you want to know more about Li’l Abner Foundation classes and activities, we invite you to click on our programs to get more information.

The Li’l Abner Foundation’s after school tutoring program is directed by a State of Florida Department of Education Certified teacher, assisted by volunteer students from the F.I.U. Honors College and Belen Jesuit Preparatory High School. Their primary function is to assist the underserved children from our community with their studies and homework assignments. Learn more about us at the Tutoring Section.

Our **Taekwondo Program** focuses on teaching the core benefits of martial arts—**self-defense, discipline, and the sport of Taekwondo**.

We provide a **fun, supportive, and structured environment** where students learn the values of **discipline, courtesy, and respect** while becoming **strong, physically fit, and self-confident**.

**Li’l Abner Foundation Archery** is an official **USA Archery JOAD Club** , proudly serving the Miami-Dade County community since 2015.

We offer a full **Junior Olympic Archery Development (JOAD)** program for youth ages **10 to 20** , and we are **now welcoming adult archers** as well.

Whether you’re brand-new or looking to advance your skills, **join us today and learn the sport of archery under the USA Archery National Training System.**

We present our renewed Dance program, which will be open every Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday. See more in our [Dance section.](https://www.lilabnerfoundation.org/dance-program/)
