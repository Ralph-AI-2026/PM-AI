We are a new club under the direction of the Santa Paula Parks & Recreation Department and the Santa Paula Police Activities League (P.A.L.). We currently offer youth and adult classes, Explore Archery Programs, and will occasionally host open range days / times.

**Wednesdays** (Pre-registration required)

  * 930am-1030am Adult Class
  * 530pm-630pm Beginner Youth Class (ages 7-17)
  * 630pm-730pm Intermediate Youth Class (ages 7-17)



**Saturdays**(Drop-in)

  * 9:00am Youth Class
  * 10:00am Adult Class
  * 11:00am Adult Class

Register for classes through the SP Parks & Recreation Department here [www.spcity.org](https://santapaula.recdesk.com/Community/Home)
