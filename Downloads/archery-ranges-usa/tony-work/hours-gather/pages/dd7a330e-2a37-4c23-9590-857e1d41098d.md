# Home | Lovettsville Game Pr

### Uniting for a Common Cause

We are dedicated to bringing together landowners, sportsmen, and community members to restore and preserve wildlife, ultimately aiming to enrich the community's sports and recreation offerings.

​

### Protecting Our Wildlife

Our goal is to safeguard wildlife through responsible stocking, enforcement of laws, feeding programs, and community engagement ensuring that everyone can appreciate and enjoy wildlife within the framework of state and local regulations.

​

### Wildlife and Community

Furthermore, the LGPA supports recreational and other activities that contribute to wildlife preservation, whether through the creation of suitable habitats or by raising public awareness.
