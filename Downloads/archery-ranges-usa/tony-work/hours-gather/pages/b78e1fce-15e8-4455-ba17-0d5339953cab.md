# Obissquasoit Bowmen Inc

The club was founded in 1951, and incorporated in 1954. **And in 2026, we’re proudly celebrating our diamond jubilee—our 75th year!**

Obissquasoit owns 53 acres of wooded land which includes three 14-target ranges, a 10 thru 60 yard sight-in/practice range and a clubhouse that features; a twelve lane, 20-yard indoor range (equipped with morrell target butts), a kitchen and restrooms.
