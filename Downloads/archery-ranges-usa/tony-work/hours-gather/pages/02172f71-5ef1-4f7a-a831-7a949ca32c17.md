# SarasotaArchers.org

Sarasota Archers

4570 17th Street, Sarasota, FL 34235

Sarasota Archers is a private club, requiring membership to enter the grounds and utilize the ranges.

New Members are Welcome: See Membership Tab

Youth Archery Lessons: Wednesdays 6:00 to 7:30pm

Adult Archery Lessons: Thursdays 6:00 to 8:00pm

Lessons are open to the public with a signed waiver. A $5 donation is requested.

Meetings are on the second Tuesday of each month at 7:00pm.
