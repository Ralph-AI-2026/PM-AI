# Home | Grit City Archery

Archery is a beginner friendly sport that you can enjoy with zero experience! At Grit City Archery, certified instructors teach safe technique in a supportive environment that’s welcoming to youth, adults, and families. Beginner classes take place in our modern, indoor facility. All gear is provided. Choose the path that fits you: ​​​

Grit City Archery is in the soft open phase and is not open for walk ins.   
All range access is by reservation only. 

At this time, shooting opportunities are limited to students enrolled in our programs. Our range space focuses on our class programs and student activities.   


We anticipate offering limited public shooting opportunities in Fall 2026. If you'd like to be notified when those become available, we encourage you to join our mailing list. 

We provide a safe, supportive, and inclusive archery learning environment. Grit City Archery is run and managed by coaches who are focused on helping you enjoy the sport and grow your skills. We believe expensive equipment isn't necessary to enjoy archery. We are a reservation only range and we focus on quality archery instruction. 

  * Beginner Friendly - Youth and adults are welcome

  * Modern Indoor Facility - Stay warm and dry 

  * USAA & Safe Sport Certified Coaches/Instructors

  * Indoor & Outdoor class offerings

  * Competitive options 




Our program aims to help archers reach their goals (recreational, competitive, or hunting oriented) through technical instruction and strong personal connections with the archers we serve. We hope to create a dynamic of fun, mutual support, and excellent technical discipline​
