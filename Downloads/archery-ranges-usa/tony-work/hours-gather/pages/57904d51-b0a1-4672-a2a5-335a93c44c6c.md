Welcome to the Chequamegon Archery Club, located in Ashland, WI.   
  
We are a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization dedicated to providing a safe and fun practice range for all archers. Our archery club offers several different practice ranges with targets from 10 to 80 yards, including a 14 target Field/Hunter course, and a 20 target 2D animal course.   
  
Our membership application is now digital. You can start enjoying all the Chequamegon Archery Club has to offer once complete. If you have any questions our e-mail is listed on the contacts page.
