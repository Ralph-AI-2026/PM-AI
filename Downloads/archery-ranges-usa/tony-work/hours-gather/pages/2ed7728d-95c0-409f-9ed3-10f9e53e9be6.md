### Northwoods Bowmen’s Club is an archery club organized in 1954 to offer the opportunity to practice recreational archery. The club has an indoor range in the city of Novato in Marin County that serves as the primary site for club events and league shoots.

###  Every Monday night we host Open Shoot Night, where anyone can visit the club and try archery for free — no equipment required. All ages are welcome, though very young children may need assistance operating the equipment, which we’ll evaluate on a case-by-case basis. This event is open to all skill levels! 

### 

**April through October:** 6:30 PM start  
**November through March:** 7:00 PM start

### The club hosts Wednesday night, weekly league shoots that last approximately 10 weeks. These shoots utilize a wide variety of targets ranging from paper flint, to 3D animals. These are friendly competitions open to all skill levels!

### Members use a wide variety of equipment, ranging from traditional long bows with cedar arrows to compound bows with sites, mechanical releases, and stabilizers.

### We hold monthly meetings on the first Monday of each month. Please visit our [Ongoing Events](https://northwoodsbowmensclub.org/events-2/ "EVENTS & SHOOT TIMES") page for more information.

### [Membership](https://northwoodsbowmensclub.org/memberships/ "TYPES OF MEMBERSHIPS") is open to anyone interested in archery.

## COVID-19 Announcement

Please note that while the state has officially declared us all as open for business, we as a club are continuing to follow some general safety guidelines regarding COVID-19.

Please excercise the following guidelines while participating within the club interior:

  * Masking while indoors is highly recommended but not required.
  * Continue to practice general distancing when possible.
  * Please continue best practices when borrowing club equipment and please sanitize your hands before and after usage of club equipment.
  * Please avoid large crowding.
  * Lastly, have fun!!
