# Shooting Park | Lenexa, KS - Powder Creek Shooting Park

Powder Creek Shooting Park is Kansas City's premier shotgun shooting facility. We were established in 1949.  
We welcome recreational shooters, competitive shooters, and sportsmen alike and offer a wide range of shotgun shooting disciplines, including sporting clays, trap, skeet, and 5-stand. We even have an archery range and a catch-and-release fishing pond! At 10,000 unique customers served annually, 3 million clays thrown per year, and immeasurable smiles had daily, Powder Creek is one of the highest volume gun clubs in the United States. Come see it for yourself today!
