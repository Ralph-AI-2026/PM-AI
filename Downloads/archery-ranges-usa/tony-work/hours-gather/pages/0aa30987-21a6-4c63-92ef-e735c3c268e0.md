# BRGC | Townsend, MT United States | Broadwater Rod And Gun Club

The Indian Creek outdoor range has ranges at 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, and 1000 yards. The Sporting Clays range and the outdoor pistol bays are also located at the Indian Creek facility.

Black Ram Archery, which is located just below the Indian Creek Range is one of Montana's best outdoor archery ranges. There are 2 ranges and a practice area located at the Indian Creek Facility.
