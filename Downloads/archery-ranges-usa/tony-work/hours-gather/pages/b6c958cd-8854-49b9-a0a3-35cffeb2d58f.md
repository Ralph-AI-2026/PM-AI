# Home - Northern Virginia Archers

**Northern Virginia Archers (NORVA)**

is a private non-profit archery club located at Fountainhead Regional Park in Fairfax Station, Virginia. Club membership is open to those interested in all aspects of archery including field archery and 3-D archery with compound or traditional bows. Crossbows are currently not permitted. NORVA opens to the general public several times a year when hosting 3-D and field shoot competitions.

## Facilities

NORVA is open year-round every day from dawn to dusk.

Our challenging 3-D range is 1.5 miles long and boasts 30 individual IBO Rinehart targets.

Our field range is 2 miles long with 28 NFAA targets. A kids’ 3-D range runs along with the second half.

Our practice ranges contain five fixed ranges with distances of 20, 25, 30, 35, and 40 yards. We also have a 60-yard long-range and a 40-yard broadhead range.

Our climate-controlled clubhouse offers an arrow saw and spinner, bow press and scale, bow vice, paper tuning, chronograph, and tools.

Our partner Archery Academy, LLC offers archery lessons from qualified instructors at NORVA.

Recurring Volunteer Opportunities

To assist in keeping our membership involved in maintaining the club, we've created a 

[Signup Genius calendar](https://www.signupgenius.com/go/10C084BAEAE23A3FBC25-61411999-2026)

.

The SignUp has twice-monthly slots available for the following tasks:

  * 3-D course blowout: 4 hours
  * Field course (14 lane) blowout: 2.5 hours
  * Clubhouse area blowout: 1 hour



This makes volunteer service a little more accessible for those who cannot attend a work party, and at the same time, gives Norva a way to document service hours.
