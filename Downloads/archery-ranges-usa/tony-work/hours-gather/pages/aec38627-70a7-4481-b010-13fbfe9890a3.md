# Stockbridge Sportsmens Club - The Stockbridge Sportsmens Club

Our sporting facilities are open year round for our members, guests and their families to use and enjoy. Members have access to our skeet fields, rifle and pistol ranges and our indoor archery range. Additionally, we have an outdoor archery range and a popular horseshoe court with a dedicated club house. Our main club house offers a spacious full service banquet facility which is open to the public and is suitable for parties, catered events, weddings and more. When you add in special events, training and education opportunities, there is truly something for the whole family. For questions about membership or requests for additional information please go to the contact page and select the option best suited for your inquiry. All inquiries are responded to promptly.
