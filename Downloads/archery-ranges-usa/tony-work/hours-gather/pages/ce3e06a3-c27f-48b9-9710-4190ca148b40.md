**Welcome to the Omro Bowhunters Website!**  
**The Omro Bowhunters Archery Club****is a non-profit organization that was established on February 5, 1954 to promote the education and sport of bow hunting and to cultivate the social relationships of good sportsmanship and good fellowship among it's members.******   
**The Club shot in many different locations throughout Omro until in 1966 they built the club we now know as the Omro Bowhunters Club. Since then two additions have been added, the latest of which has turned our Club into a 40 yard indoor 3D and spot range.**  
**OBH has been a place where people can gather to practice and improve their archery skills and help anyone interested in learning the sport. This philosophy is still in practice today as we strive to improve our sport.**
