# Archery Club - Locust Lake Village Property Owners Association

![](https://i0.wp.com/locustlakevillage.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/archery.jpg?resize=480%2C640&ssl=1)

Locust Lake Archery Members and fellow Villagers,

LL Archery Club was founded in 2011 and we can reflect proudly on our many accomplishments which include:

  * the development and maintenance of a range that currently has 10 distinct shooting lanes
  * the addition of several 3D targets that add additional interest
  * hosting various Open House events that allowed hundreds of our Villagers to try or revisit archery
  * special events that included experts in bow making, taxidermy, shooting, care of equipment, and hunting
  * hosting the qualifying for archers that utilize the Village’s DMAP program
  * hosting a local Boy Scout troop at the range



So, these are just some of the accomplishments and some memories over the past few years. Our mission is to provide a safe and fun setting and give everyone who has interest in the opportunity to try archery and join the Locust Lake Archery Club if they choose to. I look forward to once again hearing the calls **“Range is Clear & Range is Hot!” **as we begin each new season. We are accepting new members; family memberships are $25 yearly.   
If you have any questions send them to me at rbgettis@comcast.net
