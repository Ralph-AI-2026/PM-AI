# Event Venue | Bates Farm | Decatur, AL

Bates Farm invites you to a whimsical escape where fantasy meets nature. Our primitive campground welcomes car campers, vintage campers, and groups, set in a magical, nature-filled backdrop.

​

Highlights:

\- [Camping](https://www.bates-farm.com/camping): year round, large sites, WIFI at some sites

  * Bath house with towel warmer, free ice machine, free washer/dryer

  * Common area with WIFI, Roku TV with hundreds of premium channels, games, seating, microwave, Keurig, refrigerator, freezer, makerspace

  * Small Specialty Coffee Shop onsite




\- [Indoor archery range](https://www.bates-farm.com/archeryrange): year-round practice for all skill levels.  
\- [3D archery range](https://www.bates-farm.com/archeryrange) tucked in the wilderness for a dynamic challenge.  
\- [Off-grid Kids Survival Science Classes](https://www.bates-farm.com/off-grid-kids): hands-on activities for ages 6-12 

\- [Kids Archery Club](https://www.bates-farm.com): beginner-friendly and fun ways for kids to develop archery skills.  
\- [Event venue:](https://www.bates-farm.com/weddings-events) ideal for weddings, festivals, and family gatherings amid natural beauty.  
\- [Nature Academy](https://www.bates-farm.com/nature-academy) (in partnership with Shoalseer.org): hands-on classes on native plants, wildlife, and outdoor survival.

\- [Outdoor Escape Games](https://www.bates-farm.com/whimsical-wonders-workshops): Get outdoors and solve a quest across Bates Farm in an immersive blend of escape room, hiking, and scavenger hunt. 

​

​

Discover an unforgettable mix of archery, events, and nature education at Bates Farm.
