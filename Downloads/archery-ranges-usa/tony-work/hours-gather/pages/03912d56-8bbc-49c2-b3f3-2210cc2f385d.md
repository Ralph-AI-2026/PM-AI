# Turpin Outdoor Education Center | Nebraska Game & Parks Commission

### Day camps

Summer camps in June and July at the Turpin Outdoor Education Center in Lincoln are geared toward kids looking to explore the natural world and learn outdoor skills.   
  


### Tournaments

The Turpin Outdoor Education Center hosts various archery and shooting sports tournaments designed for expert and novice shooters alike.

### Workshops and classes

The Turpin Outdoor Education Center offers Hunter Education classes, hunting and trapping workshops, rifle sight-ins, firearms classes, archery fun shoots and many other educational opportunities.
