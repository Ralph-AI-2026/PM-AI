# GMA | Granite Mountain Archers | Prescott

Granite Mountain Archery Range is located at 3755 Willow Cr Road Prescott, Arizona. The range sits on approximately 64 acres and the terrain on the range offers uphill, downhill, and level shooting. 

​

Our practice range provides marked yardages from 10 feet to 80 yards, at 5 yard intervals. 

We currently hold five fun 3d shoots during the year. The Silver Arrow in April, The Mile High in May, GMA in June, The Outlaw in July, and the Big Game Shoot in August. We also offer several other novelty shoots. See the 'Fun Shoot' page for more information.

Our Annual GMA 3d series begins in April. Points are cumulative over three legs and buckles will be awarded to the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winners at the conclusion of the third leg. See the 'GMA 3D Series' page for more information.

​

We also hold a Youth only tournament in May. This 3d tournament gives Youth the opportunity to compete amongst their peers without the pressure of adult influences. See the 'Youth Archers Tournament' page for more information.

​

GMA is a working club, working members' duties include, but are not limited to, range monitoring, tournament setup, club meetings, target upkeep, work parties, setup and teardown the 3D shoots, maintain and improve the range. We recognize that some folks do not have the opportunity to work on the range. For those folks we offer a membership that allows access to the range, but at an increased dues schedule. See the membership page for more information.

​

Our club is affiliated with the National Field Archery Association, the Arizona Bowhunters & Field Archery Association and affiliated clubs throughout Arizona. This affiliation provides members with a network of archery contacts and shoots held though-out the State.

# Our Purpose

The purpose of the Granite Mountain Archers “is to join together, in good fellowship, dedicated archers of the quad city area who are interested in competitive archery and bow hunting, and who are enthusiastic in their support of organized archery.” Further, the Club is interested in promoting archery to all people that have an interest in joining the fraternity of archers. Our members have a tremendous knowledge base and skill level to help beginning archers become “dedicated archers” for recreation, competition or hunting.
