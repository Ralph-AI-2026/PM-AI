# Penn Del Archers

#### About Our Club

Beginning or experienced archers are welcome at Penn-Del Archers. We have been a member/volunteer run club promoting archery activities since 1950.

Established as a Non-Profit, we have 50 acres of woodlands, streams and fields. This is one of the largest remaining natural areas in Bethel Township.

Throughout the year we regularly host USA Archery sanctioned Field Archery events where archers can set official scores and records. We also offer casual "Fun" shoots for those looking to enjoy archery in a relaxed setting. Our facilities include a Field Archery course, a 70-yard practice field, and two 3D courses that are open all year-round.

###### What We Offer

**USA Archery Sanctioned Events:** We regularly host official USA Archery sanctioned Field Archery events where participants can set recognized scores and achieve official records in a competitive yet welcoming environment.

**Field Archery Course:** Our challenging Field Archery course features targets at varying distances set throughout our natural terrain, providing diverse shots and beautiful scenery. This traditional archery discipline tests accuracy and adaptability across different ranges and angles.

**70-Yard Practice Field:** Perfect your form and distance shooting on our dedicated practice field with target bales from 20 to 70 yards. Ideal for training, warming up before competitions, or recreational practice.

**3D Courses:** For those interested in hunting-style shooting, our two 3D courses wind through the woods with lifelike animal targets, plus an elevated platform for hunting practice.

**Member & Volunteer-Run:** Since 1950, our club has been run entirely by dedicated members and volunteers who maintain our facilities, organize events, and preserve this beautiful natural area for future generations of archers.

**Beginner Instruction Available:** New to archery? We welcome archers of all skill levels and provide beginner instruction to help you get started. We support all archery styles including compound, Olympic recurve, barebow, and traditional bows - whether you're interested in competition or just shooting for pleasure.

Enjoy world-class archery facilities in every season - spring, summer, fall, and winter!

Archers of all ages and backgrounds are welcome. Archery improves hand-eye coordination, strengthens the upper body, enhances focus and concentration. Additionally, it can be a great way to reduce stress.
