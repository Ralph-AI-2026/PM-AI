#   
Best kept secret in the High Desert!

Mojave Archers Club and Range is a 501(c)(3) Tax Exempt Organization in city of Phelan, Ca.   
Our range features a beautiful desert landscape with field, 3-D, and practice targets and we welcome   
all forms of archery.   
  
The range is open to our members 7 days a week.

Major shoots are open to the public.
