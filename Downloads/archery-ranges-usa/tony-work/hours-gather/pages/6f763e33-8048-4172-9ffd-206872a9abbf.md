# Home | maac

Located just five minutes off I-81 (Exit 57) in Pennsylvania's Cumberland County, our private members-only 153 acre facility offers hunting, fishing, recreational shooting, and more!  
﻿  
﻿Since 1949, MSA has offered the community a variety

of educational and recreational programs for all ages. 

Each year, our programs include Pennsylvania Game

Commission Hunter-Trapper Education classes,

National Rifle Association Basic and Instructor

Training, and the National Wild Turkey Federation

Women in the Outdoors Program.  
﻿  
﻿In addition to programs for adults, our youth activities

include a Children's Fishing Derby, a youth archery

program, a 4-H Shooting

Sports program, and a youth .22 program. Many of our programs are open to the

public.
