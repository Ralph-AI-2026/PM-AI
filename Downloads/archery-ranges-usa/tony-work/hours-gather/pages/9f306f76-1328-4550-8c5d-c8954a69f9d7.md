# Barr Lake State Park | Colorado Parks and Wildlife

The boat ramp is located on the east side of the reservoir and accessed through the main entrance.

The boat ramp will remain open as long as it is safe and usable for launching. Once conditions no longer allow for safe access (i.e water levels are too low), the lake will close to all boating activities. Visitors are encouraged to plan ahead and check for updates throughout the season.

For more information on Barr Lake‘s boat ramp, including mandatory boat inspections, see the [Activities & Trails](/state-parks/barr-lake-state-park/barr-lake-state-park-activities-trails) page. For more statewide boating information visit the [**CPW Statewide Boating**](/activities/boating)page.
