# Black Sheep Bowmen Archery Club - Home

The Black Sheep Bowmen Archery Club is a non-profit organization located on  
Joint Base Elmendorf Richardson in Anchorage, Alaska.  
Our membership is open to both military and civilian. We have indoor and outdoor ranges that are available to members and their guests (guests must be accompanied by an active member). We hold outdoor 3D events, hunter fun leagues, spot leagues, participate in traveling leagues and state and national competitions. We love our sport and invite everyone to join us!

The purpose and objective of the Black Sheep Bowmen shall be: to foster, expand, and perpetuate the practice of target and 3D archery, and the spirit of good fellowship among all archers; to encourage the use of the bow in hunting of all legal game, birds and animals, and cooperate with the National Field Archery Association (NFAA) in securing better hunting privileges and conditions for bow hunters; to cooperate with all conservation organizations in the propagation and conservation of game; to maintain an indoor and 3D course and conduct tournaments and games in accordance with the rules of the National Field Archery Association.
