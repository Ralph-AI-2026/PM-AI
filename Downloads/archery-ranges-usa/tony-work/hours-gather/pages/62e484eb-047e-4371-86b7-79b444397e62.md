OUR MISSION:

Established in 1969, The Chesterfield Fish & Game Club is dedicated to the purpose of the propagation of fish and game as well as the conservation, protection and perpetuation of forests, waters and minerals.  
Located in Clintonville, NY on 150 acres of Adirondack forest and trails, the club boasts a membership of over 250.

![](resources/indoor-range-1.png)

OUR FACILITIES:

INDOORS:

◦ Heated pistol and archery range.

OUTDOORS: ◦ New covered shooting shed, 75’, for pistol, rifle. 

◦ Black powder and slug range, targets/positions established. 

◦ Separate skeet range presently being groomed. Great for archery and skeet. 

◦ 3-D Archery Range; Twenty-one place 3-D Archery course. We are an 'Explore Archery Club' and we are affiliated with 'USA Archery'. Program director Jeff Gavaletz is a certified level 2 USA Archery instructor.

◦ Permanent shooting distances begin at 50’, then go to 50, 75, 100, 150, 200 and 300 yds. The shooting range is flat for the first 100 yds. There is a berm at the 100 yrd marker. Farther distance targets are located on a gently rising incline with a path suitable for vehicles adjacent to it. ◦ Clubhouse for meetings and general purpose use.
