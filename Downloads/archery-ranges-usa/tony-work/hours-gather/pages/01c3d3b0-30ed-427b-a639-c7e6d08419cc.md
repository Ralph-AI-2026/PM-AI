## I’m Coach Yale  


Head of Atlanta Archery Club  


* * *

I began my archery journey over 40 years ago, the first time I saw a recurve bow and fell in love with how cool it looked and how felt in my hand. 

  


I found a love for this practice through my journey from immaturity to a self realized adult and personal empowerment. Shooting a bow recreationally turned into instructing when I began working at my kids summer camp, and what started as an archery camp in my backyard snowballed into a club with over 200 active members and a beautiful 28 lane archery facility.

  


I believe that many of us humans unfortunately live in fear, mostly of expressing ourselves because the world and people can be cruel. Learning tools and developing the skills of a peaceful warrior (the enemy is fear) empower those who are brave enough to embrace this challenge for freedom to have courage to live their best life, an authentic life, which is a free life. 

  


We all deserve to be our authentic selves and live fearless in the world.
