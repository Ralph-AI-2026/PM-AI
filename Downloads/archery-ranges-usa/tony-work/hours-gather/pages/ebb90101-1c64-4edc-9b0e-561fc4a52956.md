# Archery Range | Everglades Archery Range | United States

Everglades Archers is a non-profit organization dedicated to the promotion of archery in all of its various forms. The purpose of our club is to educate, promote and enjoy the sport of Archery, with safety as our prime concern. We are members of the National Field Archery Association (NFAA) and Junior Olympic Archery Development (JOAD). Located in Miami-Dade County, Florida, we host a variety of archery tournaments throughout the year, including State, Field, and 3D Tournaments. Every Wednesday evening we shoot on the Flint Range under the lights, often holding organized league shoots for those who want to participate. Our youth program includes various county groups as well as Boy Scout and Girl Scout programs.
