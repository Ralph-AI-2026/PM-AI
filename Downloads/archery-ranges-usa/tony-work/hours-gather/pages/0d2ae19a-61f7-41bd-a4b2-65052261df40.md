# Davenport Valley Archers

#### Big River Conservationists “Youth Day”

##### Aug 01

###### 09:00 am - 03:00 pm

DVA sponsors the Big River Conservationists “Youth Day”. Starts at 9:00 AM to 3 PM (Check-in at 8:00 AM.) at the DVA Outdoor Range. Events include archery instruction with an archery 3D competition, BB/Pellet gun shooting, Iowa DNR, Long Grove fire truck, arts & crafts, obstacle course, plus many games!!! The Big River Conservationists (BRC) Inc. is a newly formed conservation group in the Iowa Illinois Quad Cities which is a subsidiary in itself. We are not affiliated with any national or international organization. We are a Federally registered 501(c) 3 non-profit organization. We were organized for the purpose of preserving our hunting legacy and establishing education, direction and promoting positive outdoor activities for our men, women and youth in our community, maintaining local interest in all facets of conservation and wildlife in Iowa & Illinois.
