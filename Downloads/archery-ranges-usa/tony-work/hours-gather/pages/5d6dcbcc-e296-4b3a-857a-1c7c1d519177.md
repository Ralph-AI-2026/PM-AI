**About Nenameseck Sportsmen’s Club**

Nenameseck Sportsmen’s Club is situated on 125 acres of beautiful wooded property in Hampden County and easily accessible from the surrounding towns of Palmer, Warren and Ware. We are located at 150 Bacon Road _(road access from West Ware Road)_ in Palmer, Massachusetts. 

At Nenameseck Sportsmen’s Club, we encourage men, women and children to become members whether you are new to the sport or a current or retired professional hunter, law enforcement, or military. We encourage our community of members to share their knowledge and experiences with other members to help them explore their new interests, skills, techniques and to help educate about our 2nd Amendment rights and Massachusetts Laws.

We are supported entirely by our annual memberships, donations, event funds, and maintained through volunteer work by our members. Our volunteer member committees take pride in maintaining an excellent up keep of the property, ranges and club house all year round for all our members to enjoy. 

Interested in becoming a member? Feel free to take a scenic drive out to our club grounds and check out our outdoor facilities or visit and/or participate in some of our public events, or join us at one of our monthly club meetings on the 1st Tuesday of every month.
