DSC Membership meetings for June 3rd and July 1st have been cancelled.

At its present location since 1948 the DSC occupies 106 acres on Dequindre road between M-59 and 23 mile road in Shelby Township. 

The Dequindre property is the location of our range facilities and we offer Junior programs in Archery, Rifle, Shotgun and Pistol.

Our Education Committee offers various classes including CPL Classes that are open to members and members of the community. 

At the same location you will find the office and clubhouse, with a full service members-only bar.

The DSC also has a 131 acre campground located just north of Oxford, Michigan. The campground is nestled in a scenic northern Michigan setting on the 30 acre pristine Horseshoe Lake for your camping, swimming, and fishing enjoyment. Offered are 190 campsites with water, electricity (up to 50 amp service), showers, restrooms, and a dumping station.
