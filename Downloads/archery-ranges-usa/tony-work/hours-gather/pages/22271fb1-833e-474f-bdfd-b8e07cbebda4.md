# Cos Cob Archers - A 3D & Field Archery Club Since 1954

Established in 1954, Cos Cob Archers is a members-only club situated on 25 acres just outside of Greenwich, CT. We feature a wide range of 3D targets plus numerous field targets.

In today’s crazy world, where can you get away for a few minutes or a few hours to enjoy nature and the camaraderie of fellow archery enthusiasts? Where can you go to take a pleasant walk in the woodlands, clear your head of the day's concerns, and hone your shooting skills?
