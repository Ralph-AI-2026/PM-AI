# Suffolk Archers - An organization that provides members with a unique 20-acre forest in Western Suffolk County, dedicated to archery.

Suffolk Archers is a member owned 20-acre forest that is configured into a unique combination of open field ranges and meandering trails. It is designed to enable individuals, and their families, to get the most enjoyment out of the sport of field and target archery using either traditional or compound bows. While competition is always optional, having fun is mandatory!
