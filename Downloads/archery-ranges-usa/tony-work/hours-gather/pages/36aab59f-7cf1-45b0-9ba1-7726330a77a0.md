# The Hermitage Inn Vermont I Archery Experience-The Hermitage Inn

Whether you are a beginner or an experienced archer, you can take aim and enjoy the ancient sport of archery at our on-site outdoor range.

Our experienced instructor will lead you through every step of the archery experience, including safety measures, how to hold the modern recurve bow, aiming the arrow, and firing at the static target. You will leave feeling like Robin Hood of the Green Mountains!

Suitable for all people ages 10 and above. $100 per person; 50 min session. Maximum group size 4 people. Groups of 5 or more $85 per person, 75 min session. Reservations required.
