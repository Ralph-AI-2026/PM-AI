# Hi-Tech Archery | Archery Pro-Shop & Range in Fullerton, CA

I recently drew an elk tag and it had been 4 years since I last shot a bow. I went to Hi-Tech and they were incredible helpful getting me fully set up with a new bow, site, rest, release, stabilizer, case arrows, etc. The staff was really nice and helpful making sure my draw length was spot on and my arrows were cut to the right size. The owner spent a half hour or so at the range with me making sure I was sighted in with my 20 pin (which I wasn't expecting and really appreciated because it had been so long since I last shot a bow). I'll definitely be going back to use the 4 full day range passes that came w the purchase. I totally recommend this shop if you're in the greater LA area.
