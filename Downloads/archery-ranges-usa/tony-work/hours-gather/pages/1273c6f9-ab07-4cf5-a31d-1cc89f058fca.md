# LAS VEGAS ARCHERS - Home

## **New Membership**

For those that are new or returning members that did not renew at the last yearly renewal period will be required to fill out a new member application and participate in a new member range orientation.You may complete your new member application by using this [link](https://www.cognitoforms.com/LasVegasArchers1/LasVegasArchersMembershipApplicationAsANewMemberYourFeesAreGoodThroughJune302027). Once completed please email us at lasvegasarcher@gmail.com so we may set up your orientation.  | 

## **Renewing Membership**

For those that are current members and renewing during the annual renewal period, you will not be required to participate in a range orientation.Renewal invoices are sent to the email on file starting in July of each membership year. Members who join between January and June of the membership year will not receive a renewal invoice for the following membership year. You may renew your membership by using this [link](https://www.cognitoforms.com/LasVegasArchers1/_2026RenewalLasVegasArchersMembershipApplication).
