# **Come and sling some sticks!**

Wildwood Archery offer archery experiences for all the family.

Come to the cool birch glade where you will meet under canopy to receive an introduction and instruction on how to handle and shoot a bow and arrow safely and effectively. 

You will then jump on to the practice range where you will have time to perfect your technique, have fun and gain confidence.

After a short break we will take a wander through the woods, shooting at various targets set out along the way, to test your new found skills and to experience walking in the footsteps of our ancestors. 

We’ll end our session around the fire with time for questions and to relive those perfect shots!
