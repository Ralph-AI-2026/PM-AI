# Rim Country Archery

WELCOME

RIMCOUNTRY BOWHUNTERS ASSOCIATION IS A FAMILY ORIENTED ARCHERY CLUB IN THE VERDE VALLEY OF CENTRAL ARIZONA. WE HAVE A WEEKLY SHOOTING LEAGUE AND SPONSOR THE RIM COUNTRY BOWHUNTERS SHOOT EVERY YEAR IN JULY AT CLINT'S WELL. WE ALSO HAVE A OUTDOOR RANGE FOR MEMBERS JUST OFF CORNVILLE ROAD ON THE SOUTH SIDE, .7 MILES WEST OF I17. WE ARE ALSO A 501(C)3 NON PRFIT ORGANIZATION.

OUR MONTHLY MEETINGS ARE THE SECOND MONDAY OF EACH MONTH AT 7:00 p.m. AT THE VERDE VALLEY FAIR GROUNDS..
