We are the pre-eminant J.O.A.D. program in Suffolk County. Come visit us at Smith Point Archery in beautiful Patchogue Village.  
We are the only J.O.A.D. club in Suffolk County that shoots the long distances required to be an elite archer!  
  
We teach kids and adults of all ages the fine art of Archery!  
  
Our J.O.A.D. program is among the top programs in the country with many of our archers winning medals in  
local, state and national competitions.  
  
Come down and give archery a try and experience all the benefits of a long overlooked sport.   
You will be challenged both mentally and physically as you learn to master the ancient art and modern science that is archery!  
  
You will learn to harness your core power and develop mental discipline you never thought you had and you'll find yourself applying those skills  
to all aspects of your everyday life!  
  
Archery is a sport that the whole family can enjoy together. You'll learn it in 5 minutes, but you'll enjoy it for a _**lifetime**_!!
