**_Welcome to the Silver Arrow Bowmen Archery Club, located in Mount Vernon, WA._**

**_We are a non-profit club that supports all styles of archery. Our club has traditional and compound shooters from beginners to professional and sponsored shooters._**

**_We offer an archery range (Indoor club, outdoor flat range and outdoor walk-through course with over 40 targets.)_**

**_Our club meetings are held on the 1st Monday of each month (except September), 6:15pm at the clubhouse. If you are interested in membership, stop by one of the meeting and check us out!_**
