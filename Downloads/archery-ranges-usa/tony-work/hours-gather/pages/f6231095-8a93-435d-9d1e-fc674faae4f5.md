The San Juan Archers, Inc. is a non-profit organization dedicated to growing the community of bowhunting and field archery for archers of all skill levels. We maintain two 14-target field ranges and a practice range on a 25 acre fenced-in property located northwest of Farmington New Mexico. We host the annual Chokecherry 3D Shoot in the early spring and the popular Pre-Hunt 3D shoot in August. The club holds NFAA Animal, Hunter, Field, and American Round events. The range is maintained throughout the year with targets for practice. We have access to a gym in Kirtland (courtesy of CCSD) to shoot 20 yard spot targets during the indoor season. The club has hosted NFAA Vegas and 5 Spot mail-in archery events there.
