# Kyudo

is a Japanese martial art focused on archery, emphasizing posture, breath, and intent.  
Practitioners aim for harmony between mind, body, and bow through precise, repeated practice.  
  
Etiquette, respect, and calm concentration are integral parts of kyudo practice.  
Beginners are taught basics such as grip, stance, and the sequence of movements before advancing.  
Advanced practice refines subtle aspects like timing, technique, and the expression of intent.  
Kyudo fosters mindfulness, discipline, and a lifetime commitment to improvement.
