# _Welcome, Traditional Archers!_

# [![](https://traditionalarchersofca.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/2025-TAC-LOGO-FINAL-1024x1024.png)](https://traditionalarchersofca.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/2025-TAC-LOGO-FINAL.png)

Traditional Archers of California (TAC) is a state-wide

traditional archery club. With over 150 members

throughout the state, our goal is to promote and 

perpetuate the sport of traditional archery.

We are affiliated with:

National Field Archery Association (NFAA)

California Bowmen Hunters/State Archery Association (CBH/SAA)
