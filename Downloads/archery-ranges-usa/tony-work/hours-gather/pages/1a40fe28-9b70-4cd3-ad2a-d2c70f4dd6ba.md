# Wingfoot Bowhunters Club | 425 East Waterloo Road, Akron, OH, USA

Wingfoot Bowhunters Club, established in 1926, is a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting the sport of bowhunting. Our club features a 15 to 60 yard practice range, a 30-acre field course, and tree stands, providing a perfect setting for archers of all ages to hone their hunting skills and advance their target shooting abilities. We are open year-round and welcome all archery enthusiasts to join us in our passion for bowhunting.

  
In addition to our excellent facilities, Wingfoot Bowhunters Club offers a wide range of educational courses and hosts thrilling tournaments during the summer months. Our club is committed to providing a supportive and inclusive environment for all members to enjoy and excel in the art of bowhunting.

  
Visit us at 425 E. Waterloo Rd. Mogadore, Ohio 44260 and become a part of our vibrant bowhunting community.
