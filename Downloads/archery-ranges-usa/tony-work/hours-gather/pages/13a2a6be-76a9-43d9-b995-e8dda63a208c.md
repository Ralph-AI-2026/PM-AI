The Membership at West Falls Conservation Society consists of 300 regular members, lifetime and youth members. Currently if you would like to join there is a waiting list. To add your name to this list to become a member you need to

*Attend a monthly meeting on Monday at 8:00pm. Meetings are held the second Monday of every month. Sign in. Meet new people, learn about the club. 

*Attend an orientation or “Walk About”. There is one held in the spring and fall. Here you will tour the club and grounds and find out what the club has to offer. You will be notified when the date and time is.

*When a spot opens in our membership, you will be contacted to become a member. 

Youth members (age 6-18) follow the same procedure except after the orientation, they can become a youth member the following Monday meeting.

_Three very easy steps,_ and you will enjoy yourself as well. Come visit and check us out!!

For more information you may contact us on the site and we will be sure to answer any questions you may have.
