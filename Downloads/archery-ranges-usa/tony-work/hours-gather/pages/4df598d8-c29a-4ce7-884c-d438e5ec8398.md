# Home | Evergreen Archery

Since 1962, the Evergreen Archery Club has furthered the sport through managing and maintaining an outdoor range located just outside of downtown Spokane, hosting multiple events each year, and supporting various local archery programs.

Evergreen's outdoor archery range includes a flat range with targets from 10 to 80 yards, two 14 target field ranges that span 4 to 80 yards, and a 10 target 3D range, spread out throughout the beautful forest of Eastern Washington.

​

Membership is required to access the range. Several membership options are available. See the membership page for more details.
