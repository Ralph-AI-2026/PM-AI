Our Story 

##  A Passion for Archery

Blessed Archery was started as an adventure to help grow the sport of archery in South Florida as a family fun event! We have been blessed with a love of the sport, a love for people, and a great location in South Florida. 

We are authorized dealers for **Mathews** , **Hoyt** , **PSE** , **Darton** , **Easton Arrows** , **Victory Arrows** , and **Mission Bows**. We keep almost all models in stock and would love for you to come and try them out.
