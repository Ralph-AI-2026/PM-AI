**What********is Wapiti Archers of Pennsylvania?**  


Wapiti Archers of PA is a non-profit organization located in Fort Washington State Park (PA) dedicated to the ancient and modern sport of Archery.   


  


The club maintains an outdoor practice range and two outdoor roving courses. The practice range has nine bag targets set at 5 yard increments ranging from 10-50 yards.   


  


The two roving courses are set up with fifteen to twenty 3D targets, depending on the time of year. Both the practice range and the 3D courses are open to members year round from dawn to dusk.   


  


If interested in joining Wapiti Archers of PA, you can find our membership information by [clicking here](about.html).  


**Elected Officers:**  


  


Nominations for officers takes place from October-November. Elections of officers are held at the December Monthly Business Meeting.
