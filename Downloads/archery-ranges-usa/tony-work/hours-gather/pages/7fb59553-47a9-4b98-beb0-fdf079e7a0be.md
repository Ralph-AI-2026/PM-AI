**UPCOMING CLASSES. Please call the office (704-447-8230)** during business hours**(closed Mondays) to register**  
  
**First Saturdays - Intro to Skeet Shooting/First Shots (Shotgun):** Shooters will receive on-range instruction in the finer points of skeet shooting. Bring your shotgun and 2 boxes of 7 1/2, 8, or 9 size shot, 1300 fps max. Scoring, etiquette, equipment, and camaraderie will be discussed. 8:30-9:30 AM Cost: $40—includes one round of skeet and instruction. Class size is limited to 5 shooters.   
  
**NSSF First Shots Classes:** These classes are for beginning shooters. They include classroom and range time with certified instructors. NSSF has supplied us with some goodies for these classes, your $40 fee also includes eye and ear protection. Class size is limited, so call to reserve your spot today!
