# Oklahoma Storm Joad Home Page

Welcome to the Oklahoma Storm Joad Team website: Oklahoma Storm Joad (Junior Olympic Archery Development) team is a competitive travel team of boys and girls, ages 9-19, who travel regionally, nationally, and internationalaly to compete in archery competitions. All archers on the team are from Oklahoma. We will be competing in many events this year and will update this site accordingly. ![](pics/storm2.jpg)
