# On Site Archery - Archery any time, any place

At On Site Archery, all skill levels are welcome! 

Our JOAD Club is a registered member of USA Archery, serving ages 8 to 20. We offer 2 youth groups: beginner and intermediate/tournament. All youth archers must start in the beginner group unless approved by On Site Archery staff. 

Our adult archery program (AAP) is a registered member of USA Archery. Our adult program welcomes adults of all ages and skill levels.

Club activities range from beginner instruction and recreational shooting to training for competition. We provide equipment for class use. As archers progress, they will learn more advanced shooting techniques. Both JOAD and AAP have achievement award programs, where pins can be earned in our monthly pin shoots. Each pin level achieved represents an archer’s progressing skills. We encourage all archers to participate in club activities, pin shoots and tournaments when they are ready. To participate in tournaments, archers will need to own equipment.

We also offer classes in surrounding communities. Registration for community classes is through your town recreation department website. If your school, program or business is interested in setting up an archery class or program, contact Bob.
