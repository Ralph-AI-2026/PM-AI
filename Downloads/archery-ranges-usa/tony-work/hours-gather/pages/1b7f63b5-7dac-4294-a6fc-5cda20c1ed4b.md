_**Bellator Mentium**_ \- battle of minds; balancing mind and body through archery with classical virtues - patience, kindness, light of mind, peace of heart, and will to endure anything.

We offer **[JOAD](https://www.usarchery.org/club-management/junior-olympic-archery-development-program-management)** and **[Adult Programs](https://www.usarchery.org/participate/adult)** and compete in National Events. We focus on fun but balance it with discipline, dedication, and kindness.

As BM Archers, we engage in the daily world with direction, dedication, discipline, and artistry, just as the arrows that we carry in our quivers and strike our targets.
