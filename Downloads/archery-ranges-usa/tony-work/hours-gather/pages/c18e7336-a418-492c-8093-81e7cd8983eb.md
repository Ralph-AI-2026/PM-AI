# Home | Wilcox JOAD Archery Club

  * 1st training/evaluation session is complimentary (done prior to joining the club).



*New archer(s) are by appointment only (typically on Thursdays, but based-on coach availability).

  * Complete registration forms and pay a $60 non-refundable initial registration fee (per child) and the 1st month's dues of $40 to join the club.
  * Membership is $40/mo. per individual ($30 for each additional child). Dues payable by debit/credit card on 1st of every month.
  * Register as a USA Archery member **within 90-days** of joining the club.
  * Range Fees are $12/per day or yearly membership and are **NOT** included in club dues. Range fees are payable to Wilcox Bait & Tackle (located across the street from the range - please discuss yearly range fees with the Wilcox Pro Shop).
