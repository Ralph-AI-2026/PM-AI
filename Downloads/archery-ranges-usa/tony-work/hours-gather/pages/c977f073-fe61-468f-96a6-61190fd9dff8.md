# SHASTA MOUNTAIN ARCHERS | Join Us & Shoot

We are a nonprofit organization promoting the practice of field archery and the spirit of good fellowship among all archers. 

By cooperating with the National Field Archery Association we can help secure better hunting privileges and conditions for bow hunters. Shasta Mountain Archers maintains a field course, conducts field tournaments and games in accordance the rules National Field Archery Association. We are also proud members of the California Bowhunter's Association and the National Field Archery Association.
