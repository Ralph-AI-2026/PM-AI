## _Welcome to the Chippewa Bow Hunters Website_

[![Picture](/uploads/3/4/3/6/34366831/1333131060.jpg)](/uploads/3/4/3/6/34366831/chippewabowhubtershistory.pdf)

** The Chippewa Bow Hunters Club is a family orientated archery club whose purpose is to protect, enhance, and teach the sport of Archery & Bow hunting. Founded in 1947, the CBH has promoted and taught good Bow hunting ethics for over 75 years. The club has one of the finest outdoor ranges in the state of Wisconsin, supporting over 40 acres including 30 wooded bunkers along with a state of the art practice range. We are located in the township of LaFayette. While tailoring to the hunter and the target shooters alike, our club sponsors several 3-D target shoots throughout the year.**  
We also support hunters education classes for the young archer with several certified DNR archery instructors teaching all aspects of the sport. With our annual membership of over 250 strong, our club works hard keeping our members informed through newsletters, club events, and monthly board meetings. During the summer, our Wednesday 'fun nights' provide an atmosphere where club members can relax and reminisce their favorite hunting stories and adventures. We work hard to support and preserve the sport of Bow hunting and welcome any new members who do the same.
