# Tampa Bay Sporting Clays

## How much does it cost? 

At TBSC you pay for what you use. We do not have a per-person or per-course fee. See our [detailed pricing](http://www.mytbsc.com/rates) page for more information. 

## When are you open? 

Hours : Wednesday & Thursday 8:30 am - 2:00 pm

Friday, Saturday & Sunday 8:30 am - 4:00pm 

We frequently host corporate and charity events that may close down one or more of our courses. Please check our [website calendar](http://www.mytbsc.com/calendar) for availability and course closures. Shooting typically takes 2 at least two hours, keep that in mind when deciding when to arrive. If no shooters are on site at 4:00 pm we close early on our longer days.

## What if it rains? 

We are an outdoor only facility, so weather can affect our hours of operations. If we close due to weather we will post it on our website calendar. You can also call to verify. We do not rent guns in the rain.

## Do I need a reservation?

No. TBSC operates on a first-come, first-serve basis, and you do not need to call to make a reservation. There is usually no wait time to go onto the courses; however, there may be a wait time to get a golf cart. If you have a group of 20 or more shooters, please let us know you are coming but we cannot reserve guns, carts, or pavilion space unless the group/event is under contract. See our [event reservation page](http://www.mytbsc.com/reservations) to contact us about an upcoming event.

## Do you allow handguns or rifles?

No, we only allow shotguns, and they must meet TBSC standards. No barrel length shorter than 24 inches and no tactical weapons (e.g. pistol grips, extension magazines, etc.) are permitted. If you bring a weapon that does not adhere to TBSC standards, it will be confiscated for the day, and you will be given a rental that meets the gun criteria. If this incident is repeated, you will no longer be permitted on TBSC property.

## Can I rent a gun?

Yes, you can rent a gun. We have .410-, 20-, and 12-gauge rentals. You only need one gun per group of approximately four people. We do not rent our guns in the rain. Unless you are shooting in an event, we do not take gun reservations—all guns will be rented on a first-come, first-serve basis. You must be a legal US citizen to rent a shotgun at TBSC. You will be required to provide a valid license for a gun rental, and any license left at TBSC will be returned via mail to the address listed on the license.
