# Champaign County Shooting Academy, Inc.

The Champaign County Shooting Academy is a non-profit charity serving Champaign County to promote and educate youth, college students, and adults on firearm and archery safety, marksmanship and competition.

Founded:

October 3, 2017

501(c)(3) status approved August 13, 2018

Mission:

To provide facilities to promote firearm and archery safety and marksmanship training programs focusing on youth development organizations, competitive shooting opportunities, hunter education training, access for disabled shooters and women's programs.
