# Keowee Bowmen Archery Club | We are organized to foster, expand and perpetuate the sport of archery and good fellowship among all archers

Join us for the 2026 Keowee Classic at Keowee Bowmen Archery Club on May 16th and 17th, 2026, a premier 3D archery tournament set on our scenic natural-terrain course in Central, South Carolina. This event is open to archers of all skill levels, from beginners to experienced competitors, and offers a fun, family-friendly opportunity to enjoy challenging 3D targets, great community, and a full weekend of archery.

The Keowee Classic will feature 20 targets with a single-round format, giving shooters the flexibility to compete on either Saturday or Sunday. This event is also WNC Circuit Shoot #7, making it a great stop for circuit shooters while still remaining welcoming and accessible for local archers, families, and anyone looking to spend time outdoors testing their skill.

Shooting fees are $20 for adults age 18 and older, $15 for young adults ages 15 to 17, $10 for youth ages 12 to 14, and free for cubs age 11 and under, with a $45 family maximum. Registration will be available from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM both days. Lunch will be available for purchase. The tournament director is David Thompson, and questions may be sent to keoweebowmen@gmail.com.
