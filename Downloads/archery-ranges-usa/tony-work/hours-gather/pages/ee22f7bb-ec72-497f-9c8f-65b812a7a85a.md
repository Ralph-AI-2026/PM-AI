# Ridge Archers FL - Established 1954

[![](https://ridgearchersattenoroc.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/ridge_logo-removebg-preview.png)](https://ridgearchersattenoroc.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/ridge_logo-removebg-preview.png)**Established 1954**

Ridge Archers was established in 1954, and remains one of the longest standing archery clubs in the United States. We exist to foster and perpetuate a spirit of good fellowship among all archers and stimulate the maximum development of the sport. We are located in Lakeland, FL, and are the proud home to a beautiful 3-D range, field range, and practice range at the Tenoroc Shooting Center. We hold monthly 3-D tournaments, and invite archers and enthusiasts of all ages and experience levels. Most importantly, we shoot because we love the sport and do our very best to create a fun, friendly atmosphere at all of our events.
