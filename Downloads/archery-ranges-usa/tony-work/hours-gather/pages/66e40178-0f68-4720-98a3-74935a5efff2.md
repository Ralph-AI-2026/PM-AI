# South Bay Archery Club - Archery Club, Archery

**The South Bay Archery Club** is a private, non profit 501(c)7 Archery Club located in the greater Los Angeles area along the Southern California Coast in the City of Rancho Palos Verdes. Our range is a 14-lane roving archery range with two practice areas and a broadhead tuning lane. We are also certified by the [https://www.nfaausa.com/. ](https://www.nfaausa.com/.)

  


**We have an average membership of 400 archers** of all ages, backgrounds, and bow styles. The Club is completely run by volunteer members. Our range is open to its members for use from sunrise to sunset. In addition, we host monthly field archery tournaments, periodic 3-D shoots, and have a USA Archery certified [Junior Olympic Archery Development and Adult program conducted on Saturday Mornings ](/youth-and-adult-programs)(weather permitting). 

  


Business and awards are handled at monthly membership meetings. Membership enrollment is open to the public who have their own bows.
