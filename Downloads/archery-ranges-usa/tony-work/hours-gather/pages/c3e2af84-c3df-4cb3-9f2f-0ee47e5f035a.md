**Inclement Weather Policy**

Archers Artemis does not hold outdoor classes in significant rain, thunderstorms, or high winds. We may still hold classes in light rain, or cold and hot conditions, if deemed safe for athletic activities.

If the weather is not appropriate for shooting, students and parents/guardians registered for classes on that day will receive a notice by 5PM the day before the class is scheduled. We recognize that New England weather can change quickly, and sometimes we may get it wrong. However, we prefer to err on the side of caution and provide advanced notice so that students have ample time to make alternate plans over the weekend.

If a class is cancelled due to weather, we will have make-up options available on the schedule. You will not be required to pay extra for make-up classes related to weather cancellations. Please make sure to sign up for your chosen make-up session through our system so we can plan accordingly.

If you have a private lesson scheduled, we will decide together if cancelling is appropriate, and we will schedule an alternate date accordingly.
