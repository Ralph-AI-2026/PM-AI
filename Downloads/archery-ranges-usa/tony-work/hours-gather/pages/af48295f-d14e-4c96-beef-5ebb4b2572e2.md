# Indian River Archers | Family Activities | 6000 77th Street, Vero Beach, FL, USA

Indian River Archers is a nonprofit organization established in 1989. We are committed to promoting archery while maintaining 22 acres of picturesque conservation land near the county fairgrounds. Our goal is to provide a welcoming space for archers of all ages, becoming a valuable community asset. 

​

Our club is Members Only, and we host a weekly event on Wednesday evenings (5:50-6:30) for the community to come try Archery for FREE!

Indian River Archers is dedicated to preserving tradition, fostering community spirit, and offering positive experiences for all who share a passion for archery.
