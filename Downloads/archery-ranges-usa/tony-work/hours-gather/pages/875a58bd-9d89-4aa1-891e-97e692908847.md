# Chicago's Oldest Archery Club

The Chicago Archery Club was founded in 1878 and bears the distinction of being the first chartered and oldest archery club in the United States in continuous existence. Membership in the club is opened to all above the age of eight who are willing to follow the Rules and Regulations of the Chicago Park District, CPDAA, and the club. Archery is a sport which requires an individual to set personal goals as they develop, and perfect their skills as an archer. We seek to provide others the opportunity to enjoy the sport.
