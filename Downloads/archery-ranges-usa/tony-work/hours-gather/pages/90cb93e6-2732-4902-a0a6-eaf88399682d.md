# Oranco Bowmen

Recently a four-foot gopher snake was spotted near the practice range. Please do not hurt this snake or any other gopher snakes you may encounter. Along with our barn owls, they help to manage the population of gophers around our range. Just leave it alone. The following flyer is posted in a few locations around the club. Take a minute to familiarize yourself with the difference between a gopher snake and a rattlesnake. If you see a rattlesnake, inform me or another board member of its whereabouts and keep clear of that area. Thank you for your attention to this matter. Jim Teel Range Captain (818) 317-9110
