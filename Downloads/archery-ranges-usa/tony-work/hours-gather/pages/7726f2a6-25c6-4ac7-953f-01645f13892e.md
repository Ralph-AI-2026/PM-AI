# **Cub Run Archers**

The AR-FX IWLA Cub Run Archers Club (**CRA**) is authorized by the Arlington-Fairfax Chapter of the Izaak Walton League of America to further the sport of archery and promote and establish facilities for the enjoyment of this sport by the Arlington-Fairfax IWLA membership. These facilities have been constructed so as to be in strict accordance with standards set by the National Field Archery Association (NFAA). Such facilities are located on the property of the Arlington-Fairfax Chapter of IWLA. The Club is a chartered member of both the National Field Archery Association through the Virginia Field Archery Association and the Virginia Bowhunter’s Association.
