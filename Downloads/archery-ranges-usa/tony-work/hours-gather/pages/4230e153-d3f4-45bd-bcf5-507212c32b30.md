# Possum Hollow Sportsmen's Club

Possum Hollow Sportsmen’s Club is a non-profit organization that was first established in 1956 as a one room club on 18 acres of property located in Wampum, Pennsylvania. We now boast almost 50 acres that our club members and guests alike enjoy the use of for activities such as our 3-d archery courses, the out-door muzzleloader course, rifle and pistol range and the trap house. We are conveniently located next to over 100  
acres of Pennsylvania public game lands (#146).

Numerous improvements have been made to the buildings over the years, one of the largest improvements being the 40′ x 120′ in-door archery range, so we now have even more space for club activities.

We are proud to say that we are able to continue to give back to the community in the way of monetary donations to our local church, Clinton United Methodist, to help feed the hungry at the holidays and to our local fire department to help ensure the safety of our community.

It is Possum Hollow Sportsmen’s Club’s wish to continue to promote the shooting sports and to provide adults and youth alike, whether they be avid sportsmen or inexperienced newcomers to the shooting sports, continued enjoyment of our facility for many years to come. We encourage the public to visit us to see what has captured the interest and the hearts of our members, some of whom have been with us for well over 25 years.
