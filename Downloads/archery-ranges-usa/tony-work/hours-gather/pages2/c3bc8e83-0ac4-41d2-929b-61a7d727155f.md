# Swift River Sportsmans Club, Inc. - Hunting, Sportsmans Club, Fishing

We are a private sportsman's club with 400 acres of land located in Belchertown, MA. We are also 'next door' to Herman Covey WMA, with 1474 acres of public hunting land. Our property offers excellent hunting for deer, turkey and other upland game. The club stocks pheasant and quail for members' hunting.

  


Along with the wooded area we also own extended frontage along the Swift River, one of the best cold water fisheries in the state. The crystal clear water is well stocked by the state. The public boat launch adjacent to our property allows easy access for small craft. Anglers can expect to find everything from perch to bass and large trout. Our river front property has a few sites for camping.

  


The Club is easily accessed from any direction by main roads. We are within easy driving distance from Worcester, Springfield, Amherst and Northampton. We're also a short drive from the scenic Quabbin Reservoir, the state's McLaughlin Fish Hatchery and the Massachusetts Division of Fisheries and Wildlife District Headquarters.
