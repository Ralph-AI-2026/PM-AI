# Home

Fort Lauderdale archers is a private, non-profit organization dedicated to promoting the sport of archery. Located at 4590 Peters Road (just west of State Road 7/441), and only minutes from most areas of Broward County, this 40-acre, 5-star rated club features a 90-meter FITA range, a lighted practice range consisting of targets from 10-yards to 80-yards, three national 3-D courses, a 28-target field course and a FITA / 900 Round competition course.

Member, Participating Member and Full-time Student memberships are available. After becoming a registered member, you are provided with the key to the main gate, offering you 24-hour, 7-day a week access to the club. Lighting is accessible as well for those who enjoy shooting at night.

Whether you’re a first-time archer, a seasoned competitor, or a family looking for an active outdoor experience, our club provides the resources and community to help you learn, practice, and enjoy the art of archery. We are committed to safety, sportsmanship, and fostering a lifelong passion for this timeless sport.
