**T-Town Drillers JOAD**

****Junior Olympic Archery Development Program (“JOAD”)****

JOAD is a nationwide program of USA Archery that provides kids and teens with ongoing archery classes that help them progress from beginner to advanced level archery. The JOAD team is open to anyone aged 8 to 20 and is designed to grow with the archer but all ages are invited to come shoot and learn!

JOAD Star Pins are awarded for scoring achievements. JOAD archers can earn Stars Pins for shooting indoors and/or outdoors with recurve and/or compound bows. JOAD archers can also train to compete in local and regional tournaments, and can earn a seat on a youth world team to compete in other countries representing the United States on behalf of USA Archery.

Archery Outpost’s T-Town Drillers JOAD club is excited for having Mike Reed, a Level 4 certified USA Archery Coach, coaching our Club. Mike has extensive experience in the JOAD program, and has successfully coached nationally ranked and National Grand Champion archers.

The JOAD program will meet from 9:00 to 11:00 Saturday Mornings with all JOAD the participants for general archery instruction and range time and Thursday evenings will be available for focused coaching training sessions for the more advanced archers in the club.

All prospective members must provide proof of prior Archery training or must complete the Introduction to Archery course ($20) to enroll in JOAD at Archery Outpost.
