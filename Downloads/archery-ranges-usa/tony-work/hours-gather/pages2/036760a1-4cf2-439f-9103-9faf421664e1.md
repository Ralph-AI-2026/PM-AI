# Welcome to Londonderry Fish & Game Club

![lfgc logo sm](https://www.londonderryfishandgameclub.com/images/stories/lfgc-logo-sm.jpg)LFGC, was established in 1951 for the purpose of promoting conservation, perpetuating safety and ethics through Hunter Education Programs, promote respect for land through goodwill and respect, and for the ethical and proper use of our natural and wildlife resources. [googleb4c1971f953a9e7a.html]  


Londonderry Fish & Game Club maintains the following shooting ranges including:
    
    
    50 yrd Rifle Range
    50 yrd Training Range
    Axe & Knife Throwing
    100 yrd Rifle Range
    200 yrd Rifle Range
    400 yrd Rifle Range
    
    
    25 yrd Utility Range
    25 yrd Training Range
    15 yrd Training Range (SOON)
    50 yrd Utility Range
    Shotgun Range with 3 Throwers
    50 yrd Archery Range

Beyond our ranges we have a fully stocked trout pond if you want to fish and 82 acres of land you can hunt and hike on. We also abut to 1000 acres of [Musquash Conservation Area](https://londonderrytrails.org/musquash-conservation-area/) and 500 Acres of [Litchfield State Forest](https://stateparks.com/litchfield_state_forest_in_new_hampshire.html).

**The Londonderry Fish & Game Club promotes:**

  * Conservation, safety and ethics through Hunter Education Programs
  * Community stewardship through events offered to community and local organizations
  * Respect for land through the goodwill and respect by men and women sportsmen
  * Ethical use of our natural and wildlife resources



Londonderry Fish and Game Club, in its' commitment to advocacy of hunting, fishing, trapping and conservation strives to partner with like minded charities committed to bring wildlife and outdoor education as a component in quality of life. Londonderry Fish and Game Club welcomes the support of charitable contributions to make these events possible. Please consider supporting the LFGC through club membership, a monetary donation, or other goods or services in helping us make these events possible.

All donations no matter how big or small are, graciously accepted.
