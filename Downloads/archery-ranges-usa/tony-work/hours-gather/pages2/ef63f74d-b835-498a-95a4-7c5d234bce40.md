# Southwest Bowhunters

This is a Halloween-themed novelty shoot, consisting of twenty (20) 3D targets, and takes place in the Canyon Course. _****_ Bring your FLASHLIGHTS, SPOTLIGHTS, HEADLAMPS, etc. , and LIGHT UP THE NIGHT!! Binoculars are welcome. Beware of the ZOMBIES!!!

Refreshments and food available at concession stand.

There will be awards for 1st place in the following categories:

  * Men’s and Women’s Compound
  * Men’s and Women’s Traditional
  * Young Adult (13– 17 yrs) Boy’s and Girl’s Compound
  * Young Adult (13 – 17 yrs) Boy’s and Girl’s Traditional
  * Youth (10 - 12 yrs) Boys & Girls
  * Cub (6- 9 yrs) Boys & Girls
  * Pee-Wee (5 & Under) Boys & Girls



_**Costume contest for KIDS 12 & UNDER! Prizes for the SCARIEST, CUTEST and the MOST ORIGINAL costumes. **_

Novelty targets for both kids and adults on lighted range. 

Registration begins at 4:00pm; Kids costume contest - 5:00pm; Archers' meeting - 5:45pm; Shotgun Start - 6:15pm. 

Adults - $20; Families - $40; Youth (17 & under) - $15.
