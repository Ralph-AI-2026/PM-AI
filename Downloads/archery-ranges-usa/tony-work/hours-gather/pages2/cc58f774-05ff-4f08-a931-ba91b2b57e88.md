## Welcome to  
​Fremont Archery Club

​The mission of Fremont Archery Inc. is to promote and teach all aspects of archery to athletes, children,  
and people of all ages without discrimination by programs and opportunities which educate the public on all aspects of the sport of archery,   
including but not limited to target shooting at local, national, and international archery competitions;   
and use of archery in the sport of legal and ethical hunting.
