# Fredericksburg 3D Archery

### Start the season off right in our 2026 FBG3D Shooter of the Year Buckle Series! 

You must shoot a minimum of 3 shoots AND the season finale BBQ shoot.

_-if you shoot more than 3 your top 3 scores will be used._

All scores must be in the same class.

Class must have a minimum of 3 shooters to be eligible.

_-this is 3 shooters with all 4 eligible scores across the season_

Your top 3 scores and the BBQ shoot score will be combined and averaged for your overall score.

**The highest overall score in each class wins.**

In the event of a tie, we will have a shoot-off at the final shoot of the season.

_*_ **_Note_** _: Due to smaller class sizes for the women’s classes we want to make it possible for them to compete for a buckle as well so we have agreed to combine those classes for the buckle points to make every woman that shoots eligible._
