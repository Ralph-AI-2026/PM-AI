# Usery Mountain Archers - Archery, Bow & Arrow

Welcome to **Usery Mountain Archers Club** official website. We try to serve the Archery community by providing timely information about current events and issues concerning archers and archery. 

Usery Mountain Archers is a small group of archers from around the valley promoting 3D and Field archery events held at the Usery Mountain Park archery range located at: 

3939 N Usery Park Rd. Mesa, AZ 85207 

(8 miles north of US60 on Ellsworth Road).

Compound and recurve bows welcomed!

  


Club dues are $25.00 per year for adults or $40.00 for a family membership.
