# Timpanogos Archers - Utah's Premier Archery Club

We’re a club serving hunters, competitive shooters and archery enthusiasts throughout Utah. We take no small degree of pride in having the best outdoor archery range in the state and one of the best in the US. 

Come join our thriving family-friendly archery community. The gates are open Wednesday and Thursday evenings through the spring and summer. Hone your skills and enjoy time with friends. Your first day at the range is free.

We have over [90 targets from 10-80 yards spread over 6 courses](https://timpanogosarchers.com/range-map/). For a more authentic experience our 3d courses provide a fun and challenging shoot. If you are the competitive type come join our shooting leagues on Wednesdays & Thursdays: May – Aug.

To help maintain the integrity of the road, please engage 4×4 and do not drive quickly. Trail etiquette says that the driver going uphill has the right of way, but it isn’t possible in every situation. If you are leaving the range yield to those coming up. Please use common sense and common courtesy.
