##  Kazden Ammons 

**Shatterproof Archery started without me**. My brother Kramer Ammons reluctantly offered bowstrings after his wife told him that people would be interested. He sold 3. After a couple of months, a DIY Bow building video went viral and he sold hundreds. Kramer asked me if I would be willing to stand in the gap building bowstrings so that he could continue making YouTube videos. 

**I bought Shatterproof Archery.** After working with me for over 4 years, Kramer was ready for whatever was next (a lot and a lot of miles of running. If your interested in what Kramer is doing, you can check it out here at 55miles.com)

**I help people help more people enjoy more archery.** Yes you read that right, my endeavor has been to create a team that can help more people enjoy more archery. And we are really good at it. If you don't believe me, test us.

Stay Shatterproof!
